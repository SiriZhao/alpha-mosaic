# Roadmap

The platform now includes daily and calendar-week research, qualified FIFO/asset accounting and a bounded offline research workflow. This is still not complete production acceptance. See docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md for dependencies and gates.

Next P0/P1: licensed adjusted/raw-action snapshots, historical universe and delistings; independent golden datasets; payment-date dividends and cash-in-lieu; broader failure/property tests and independently qualified real cases. FIFO matching and additive asset accounting are implemented within the documented model; preserve abstention until data qualifies.

Next P2: stronger benchmark/tax/return-basis choices, predeclared regime/ablation workflows exposed in the terminal, full experiment-comparison/rolling-risk charts, safe interrupted-plan reconciliation and broader UI flows. Weekly resampling/annualization/decisions, bounded queues, queued cancellation and per-home cross-process experiment leases are implemented. PDF/Parquet export still needs verification.

Next P3: optional model-driven hypothesis planning and richer statistical criticism beyond the implemented deterministic frozen-grid workflow, with independently checked advanced multiple-testing definitions. Sector constraints need qualified mappings; full covariance optimization requires robust estimation and validation. No performance-driven automatic strategy promotion is planned.

No live trading, leverage, shorting, options, automatic strategy promotion, paid cloud or personal-account connectors are planned under this research-only boundary.
