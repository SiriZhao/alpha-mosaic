# Daily cash-equity simulation assumptions

Explicit weekly opt-in `weekly_feature_policy=RAW_SPLIT_PRICE_ONLY_V1` supplies separately typed, split-only derived closes to registered weekly rules. For week close t and the last complete available week L, divide raw close(t) by the product of split ratios with t < effective_date <= L, known before the effective open and decision. Ordinary dividends are excluded from this price hypothesis. Raw bars still determine fills, volume, marks and cash/share/receivable accounting. The default `LEGACY_REJECT_RAW_ACTIONS` guard remains. Derived evidence is not a quote or certified PIT; special/due-bill, ambiguous same-day split/dividend and late/uncertified terms remain refused. Complete-week timing and next-open execution are unchanged.

Continuation: decision frequency can be DAILY or explicit WEEKLY. Weekly decisions occur only after the actual natural-week final exchange session; all execution, liquidity and cash accounting remains daily. Weekly rule parameters are recorded independently and cannot be replaced by an unrecorded strategy-constructor override. Unknown/unreviewed RAW corporate actions block weekly rules and source views rather than silently dropping a week. Daily methods remain available under their documented limitations.

Execution evidence now supports separate FIFO matching analysis and additive asset accounting. It does not change the original ledger or engine's moving-average cost display. The experiment is rejected if the independent reconstruction cannot reconcile the complete XNYS grid, every signal/order/fill/action/cash record, positions, marks and NAV. No historical artifact is retrospectively rewritten to add these statistics.

Universe: explicit USD stocks/ETFs; long-only, no leverage, no options or brokerage. One XNYS session grid, finite positive OHLC and required volume; unaligned/missing sessions are rejected instead of receiving ideal fills. Inactive/delisting/symbol-change economics without reliable support are rejected. A current surviving basket is not an unbiased historical universe.

Timing: close observations become strategy history only when available. Close-generated signals create proposals; orders become eligible at a later session open (default one session, configurable 1..20). Pending reservations prevent overselling. Fills link to original order and signal identifiers. Changing future bars must not alter earlier signals/fills. Warmup history may precede evaluation; no simulated positions/cash are imported from training.

Execution: opening price plus adverse per-side slippage and half of configured full spread; commission is basis points of filled notional. Round quantities to configured lot, reject/unfill infeasible quantities, and cap participation at a fraction of **the day's completed volume**. This last field is an ex-post capacity assumption, not information available at the opening auction. Daily bars cannot validate executable liquidity or market impact. Pending orders have explicit disposition rather than guaranteed 100% ideal execution.

Default ReplayConfig: USD100,000 initial cash, 10bp commission, 5bp slippage, zero explicit spread, 1% daily-volume participation, lot1, no borrowing/shorts, maximum target gross1 and maximum single target1. Strategy Lab and benchmark cases use named `realistic`: 10/5/2bp commission/slippage/full spread with 2% cash buffer. `stress`:25/25/10bp, 5% cash, 0.5% volume. `zero` is a control. These scenarios jointly change cost/cash/capacity; a causal cost-monotonicity claim needs an identical fill path and other assumptions held fixed, as tested separately.

Portfolio constraints cap targets, retain residual cash and bound new buys using opening valuations and after-cost NAV. Limited sells cannot fund invented buys. Market drift after fills can exceed target weights or cash percentages; diagnostics record this. The system does not claim continuous intraday rebalancing, sector enforcement or full risk parity.

Accounting schema2: NAV = cash + sum(quantity × close) + unpaid dividend receivables. Ordinary cash dividends earn a fixed non-spendable right at sourced ex-open before fills; modeled cash becomes available at the first XNYS open strictly after the sourced payment date. Payment reclassifies assets and does not recognize income twice. Sold shares and intervening splits do not change the original claim. Dates/ordinary type/share basis/source ID and timely terms are required; unknown, special/due-bill and ambiguous same-day split/dividend cases reject. Face-value collection/no taxes/default/impairment is an explicit simulator assumption, not observed broker behavior.

Buy/sell cash changes by signed filled notional plus commission; spread and slippage are already inside fill price and must not be debited twice. Receivables cannot fund buys or cash buffers. Average acquisition cost includes fees. Splits scale shares and inverse-scale average costs; pending order revisions are append-only. Fractional holdings remain held; no cash-in-lieu. Adjusted/total-return inputs cannot also receive duplicate cash dividends. Old missing-field ReplayConfig records remain schema1 with legacy immediate cash credit; those artifacts are not rewritten. New research defaults/presets explicitly choose schema2. Legacy dividends cannot qualify strict real replay, and comparison gates separate the models. See docs/DIVIDEND_PAYMENT_ACCEPTANCE_20261025.md.

No interest/financing/tax/FX/borrow model. No delisting returns, opening-auction certification, securities-lending benefits or real-account synchronization. Simulated orders are research proposals only.
