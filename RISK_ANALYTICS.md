# Return and risk conventions

Under payment accounting schema2, stock market exposure uses marked stock value/NAV; unpaid dividend claims are a separate receivable_weight, alongside cash_weight and market_weight. HHI includes squared stock/ETF weights in total NAV, excluding cash and claims. Missing/inconsistent marks or claims prevent qualified concentration rather than inventing values. FIFO economic P&L includes earned dividends, including unpaid amounts; paid and outstanding dollar amounts and entitlement allocations are disclosed separately. It is not cash-realized or tax-lot profit. Legacy/payment accounting mismatch blocks daily/weekly benchmark statistics and pooled experiment comparisons. See docs/DIVIDEND_PAYMENT_ACCEPTANCE_20261025.md.

Risk Lab presents persisted daily rolling statistics, cash/NAV, NAV-weighted HHI, drawdown episodes and reconciled asset/cost records. Each new exposure grid includes every equity session: known flat positions reconcile to HHI0/cash weight1, while incomplete or inconsistent marks leave concentration null. Legacy records are aligned only for display, with missing values retained. Risk-free/annualization/window assumptions and chart provenance remain explicit; full simulation configurations distinguish preset changes from a fee-only counterfactual. See docs/TERMINAL_RISK_ACCEPTANCE_20261025.md.

## Qualified execution analysis

Version2 experiment artifacts run evaluation.trades.analyze_trades against both replay events and the selected versioned dataset. Only VERIFIED reconciliation fills matched-trade metrics; inconsistent events, actions, marks, cash, sessions or identities fail the experiment and preserve the rejected result. Standalone return-series analytics still leave unmatched-trade fields null when no execution evidence has been supplied.

trade_count and closed_lot_match_count count FIFO sell-to-entry matching segments; completed_round_trip_count separately counts positive-to-flat position episodes. Partial sells split entry/exit fees and posted dividends proportionally. Win rate uses positive closed-segment net P&L including allocated posted dividends; breakeven is not a win. Profit factor is positive net closed P&L divided by absolute negative net P&L and is null when there are no losses. Open positions never become invented wins/losses. Holding period averages complete exchange-session differences, weighted by closed entry cost basis.

Splits rescale lot quantities and preserve total cost; dividends remain separate income. Fill prices already include slippage/spread, so these are not debited again. Asset realized-price P&L + unrealized-price P&L + dividend income reconciles additively to ending NAV minus initial cash. This FIFO decomposition differs from the engine's displayed moving-average costs, while total economic P&L is unchanged. It is accounting contribution, not causal factor attribution or tax-lot advice.

Complete-week NAV analysis uses52 periods/year and keeps partial initial/final intervals separate. The weekly metric return covers complete weeks; inception_return_through_last_completed_week includes the initial partial interval. Week-end sampling misses intraweek losses, so the daily risk report is retained. The NAV available_at field is a simulated valuation timestamp, never proof of provider availability/PIT certification. Source WeeklyBar.available_at separately preserves component observation availability.

Core implementation: `evaluation/analytics.py`; tested independently from optional QuantStats. Daily simple return r_t = NAV_t/NAV_(t-1)-1, with initial cash as NAV_0. No external capital flows. Exactly252 exchange observations/year; weekly annualization is not implied. Missing/nonfinite returns are rejected; undefined ratios are null.

Total return = product(1+r)-1. CAGR = product(1+r)^(252/n)-1. Sample volatility = std(r,ddof=1)×sqrt252. Sharpe uses mean daily excess divided by sample standard deviation×sqrt252; annual risk-free0 is the explicit default, not a sourced Treasury series. Sortino uses sqrt(mean(min(excess,0)^2)) over **all** periods. Calmar = CAGR/abs(max drawdown) when denominator is nonzero.

Drawdowns include initial capital in the high-water mark; duration counts exchange observations underwater. Episodes expose recovery or an unrecovered end. Rolling volatility/Sharpe and, for compatible aligned benchmarks, beta/correlation/active statistics use trailing windows.

Beta = sample covariance(strategy,benchmark)/sample variance(benchmark). Annual descriptive alpha =252×(mean strategy−beta×mean benchmark), an arithmetic zero-RF OLS intercept. Tracking error =std(active,ddof1)×sqrt252; information ratio =mean(active)/std(active)×sqrt252. These regressions are associations, not causal factor attribution or independent evidence of excess returns.

Benchmark comparisons require matching dates, declared return basis and evidence class. Unknown/unequal price, total-return or raw-plus-cash-action bases are blocked; reports omit incompatible benchmark traces. Whole-experiment comparisons additionally require the same dataset/source hash and validation/benchmark metadata. Different cost models remain explicitly visible for sensitivity review.

Costs: commission/slippage/spread in USD, plus explicit cost sum / initial capital. This cost-drag measure is not a counterfactual strategy-return difference. Turnover follows engine notional accounting. Market exposure averages1−cash/NAV; HHI sums squared invested-asset weights and reports cash separately. Closed-trade count, win rate, profit factor and average holding period are **null** until a matched-lot engine exists; fill_count is a separate execution count. Asset P&L and cash-interest attribution are also null. No fabricated factor-return series or causal explanations.
