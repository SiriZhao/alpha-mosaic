# Roadmap

The current deliverable is a locally verified daily research milestone, not complete production acceptance. See docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md for dependencies and gates.

Next P0/P1: licensed adjusted/raw-action snapshots, historical universe and delistings; independent golden datasets; payment-date dividends and cash-in-lieu; matched closed lots, asset/cash contributions; broader failure/property tests. Preserve abstention until data qualifies.

Next P2: audited weekly resampling/annualization, stronger benchmark/tax/return-basis choices, predeclared regime/ablation workflows exposed in the terminal, full experiment-comparison charts, long-job cancellation and robust multi-session quotas. PDF/Parquet export only with verification and a real need.

Next P3: small controlled planner with durable tool audit, optional provider-neutral model adapter, explicit global quotas/deadlines; advanced multiple-testing statistics only after independently checked definitions. Sector constraints need licensed mappings; full covariance optimization requires robust estimation and validation. Neither is added to inflate feature count.

No live trading, leverage, shorting, options, automatic strategy promotion, paid cloud or personal-account connectors are planned under this research-only boundary.
